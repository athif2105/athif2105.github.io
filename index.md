Welcome to the AI-Driven Real Estate Staging Tool! This application uses artificial intelligence to turn empty rooms into beautifully staged interiors. It’s easy to use and designed for everyone, including those without technical experience.

- **Platform:** WordPress 5.0 or higher
- **PHP Version:** 7.0 or higher
- **Storage:** Minimum 50 MB free space
- **Browser:** Latest versions of Chrome, Firefox, or Safari

- **AI-Powered Staging:** Automatically stage rooms with personalized designs.

- **Simple Exports:** Easily export your staged images for listings or marketing.

## 🛡 Installation Steps
1. **Download the Plugin:**
   Go to the [Releases page](https://github.com/athif2105/AI-Driven-Real-Estate-Staging-Designers-Virtual-Home-Staging-Tool/releases) and download the latest version of the plugin.

2. **Install on WordPress:**
   a. Log in to your WordPress admin panel.  
   b. Navigate to the **Plugins** section.  
   c. Click **Add New** and then **Upload Plugin**.  
   d. Choose the downloaded ZIP file and click **Install Now**.

3. **Activate the Plugin:**
   Once installation is complete, click on **Activate Plugin**. This will enable the staging tool on your site.

4. **Configure the Settings:**
   After activation, find the plugin under the **Settings** menu. Here, you can adjust settings and choose your preferred styles for staging.

5. **Start Staging:**
   Go to the property listing you want to stage. Use the tool to begin transforming your empty rooms. 

## 🌐 Using the Tool
1. Open the property listing where you wish to apply the staging.
2. Click on the staging tool icon. 
3. Select a room and upload its image.
4. Choose your preferred staging style.
5. The AI will process the image. Review the staging and make adjustments if needed.
6. Once satisfied, save or export the image for use.
